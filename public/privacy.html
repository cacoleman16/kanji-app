Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Short version: Kanjido stores your study progress on your device. We don't run a backend, we don't track you, we don't sell ads, and your kanji review history never leaves your device — except when you choose to back it up to your own iCloud or export it to a file you control.

What we collect

Kanjido is built around an on-device-only model:

  • Study progress and SRS state (which cards you've reviewed, your ease/interval/due times, daily counts, streaks) is stored in your device's local browser storage.
  • Custom decks you create (deck names and card content you typed or imported) is stored in the same local browser storage.
  • Settings (daily goal, theme, font size) is stored locally.

Kanjido does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifiers, or any other personal information.

What we don't do

  • No analytics SDKs. No third-party trackers.
  • No advertising or ad-targeting partners.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of your data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.

Subscriptions and payments

Kanjido Pro (monthly and yearly) is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment processing — we never see your card number, Apple ID, or billing address. We receive only an anonymous receipt confirming active entitlement, validated through Apple's StoreKit and our subscription provider, RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not associate purchase status with any personally identifying information. The only piece of data we store on-device tied to Pro is an opaque entitlement flag and an anonymous customer ID assigned by RevenueCat.

iCloud backup (when running as the iOS app)

On the iOS native build, Kanjido offers an optional iCloud Drive backup of your study progress. When you turn this on, a JSON snapshot is written to the Kanjido folder in your own iCloud Drive. It is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple, and only your Apple ID can read it. Kanjido cannot read the contents of iCloud users' accounts. Turn this off in Settings at any time and the local copy is the only one that exists.

Children

Kanjido is rated 4+. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The on-device data model means there is no server-side profile to delete in any case.

Your rights

  • Export all your data at any time as a JSON file: Settings → Export progress.
  • Delete all your data at any time: Settings → Delete all my data, or just uninstall the app. There is no separate deletion request to send because there is no server-side copy.
  • Manage subscriptions in App Store → Settings → Subscriptions.

Required-reason API disclosures

For Apple's privacy manifest, Kanjido uses the following APIs for the following reasons:

  • UserDefaults (CA92.1) — to persist your study progress and settings on-device.
  • File timestamps (C617.1) — to manage the iCloud backup file when present.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material about how Kanjido handles data, we'll update this page and bump the "Last updated" date above. Substantive changes (e.g. adding analytics) will surface in-app on the next launch.

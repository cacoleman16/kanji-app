What Kanjido is

Free vs Pro tiers

The free tier includes the JLPT N5 starter deck and one custom deck (up to 50 cards). Kanjido Pro unlocks the full default-deck library, unlimited custom decks and cards, Anki .apkg import, and iCloud backup on the iOS app. The exact list of Pro features may change over time as the App grows; we won't move features that were previously free into the Pro tier without notice.
